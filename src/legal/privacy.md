# Folio Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Developed by Hope Nkuna, Pretoria, South Africa

## The short version

- Folio works on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- Your files are never uploaded to us. We do not receive, see or store them.
- What you create stays on your device until you choose to share it.

## Who we are

Folio is a PDF toolkit for Android and Windows, developed by Hope Nkuna (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at {{EMAIL}}.

## What Folio does with your files

When you open a PDF or Word document in Folio, it is processed on your device. Folio keeps the following on your device so the app can work:

- **Library:** results you save, such as a merged PDF. On Android these sit in the app’s private storage. On Windows they sit in a Folio folder inside your Documents folder.
- **History:** a copy of each document you open, so you can reopen it later. Folio keeps up to 25 recent documents, or 200 MB, and removes the oldest first. On Android this is in the app’s private storage. On Windows it is in the app’s data folder.
- **Scan drafts (Android):** pages you have scanned but not yet saved, kept so you do not lose them if the app restarts.
- **Small preferences:** for example, which tools you use most, so the home screen can show shortcuts.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else unless you share it.

## Information we collect

None. We do not run servers that receive your files or your activity. Folio contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service. Because we hold no personal information about you, we have nothing to sell, rent or disclose.

## Android permissions

- **Camera:** used only when you tap Scan. Scanning is done by Google’s on-device document scanner, which is part of Google Play services. Google’s own terms and privacy policy apply to that component.
- **Files:** you choose files through the system file picker. Folio does not browse your storage.
- **Internet:** Folio does not use the internet to send your files or your usage to us.

## Sharing

A file leaves Folio only when you tap Share. It goes to the app you choose, and that app’s own privacy policy then applies. On Windows, “Show in folder” and “Save a copy” put files only where you choose.

## Third parties

- **Google Play services** on Android, for scanning, as described above.
- **Our website and downloads:** the Folio website may load fonts from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address and browser details when you visit. Downloads are hosted on GitHub, whose privacy statement applies when you download.

The apps themselves load nothing from third parties.

## Your control and keeping data

You can delete any file from the Library, remove items from History or clear it entirely, and discard unsaved scans, all inside the app. Uninstalling Folio on Android removes everything it stored. On Windows, uninstalling does not remove files in your Documents\Folio folder or the app’s data folder, so delete those yourself if you want them gone.

## Security

Your files are protected by your device’s own security, such as your lock screen and device encryption. Folio does not add its own encryption. Please note that watermarking or the Optimize tool does not securely redact information from a document.

## Children

Folio is not directed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you email us, we use your message only to reply, and you may ask us to delete it. We aim to handle any personal information in line with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and mention material changes in the release notes.
